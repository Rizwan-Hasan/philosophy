The human brain cannot accept that life is meaningless. In search of meaning, we discover gods and create religion. Under the guidance of spiritual leaders, we can shed blood in the name of these gods. Followers of one meaning can kill those who hold different meanings. If this is the meaning god blesses his followers with, then it is time to question those spiritual leaders, because killing others for having different meaning cannot be a true meaning of life.
