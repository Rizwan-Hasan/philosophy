title: "Faith in the Absence of Certainty"
date: 2026-06-12
tags: [reality, observation, life, religion, god, faith]
disqus: false
---

Worship seems to require faith without certainty, a commitment to believe despite many unanswered questions. If truth and falsehood are only human perceptions, then complete knowledge might be impossible to achieve. In such a world, believing in any entity is, to some extent, an act of blindness rather than certainty.
